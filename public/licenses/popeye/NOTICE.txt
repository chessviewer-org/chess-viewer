Popeye 4.103
https://github.com/thomas-maeder/popeye
Upstream commit: 8bf65b054482cae04fc36e07c6b880fb4f93c673 (v4.103)

Built from the complete upstream C sources with Emscripten 6.0.13.
The patches and position-preview.h in the included
popeye-4.103-build-inputs.tar.gz add board snapshots and move history to the output for interactive
previews. history-context.patch also supplies the preceding played move vector
to the native SynchronMover and AntiSynchronMover root checks when a new
interactive diagram is submitted. Native search and movement rules are retained.
The browser worker and application integration are ChessViewer code.

Popeye's distribution terms are in the included original py-engl.txt, headed
"Some remarks on the distribution of popeye". In particular, upstream requires
distribution in its entirety. The complete upstream repository contents at the
commit above, including manuals, examples and tests, are distributed here as
popeye-4.103-source.tar.gz (SHA-256:
34eaa1c5d465462553367c0331d46e3a268029eb990f151bee5e4764477b0929).
The same revision is available at
https://github.com/thomas-maeder/popeye/tree/8bf65b054482cae04fc36e07c6b880fb4f93c673
ChessViewer's patches, header, and build recipe are in
popeye-4.103-build-inputs.tar.gz (SHA-256:
b95fb824157ea090e5ffb523fc68b1cf479b37aa478d5bf6e028a95cf8a6a204).
Extract it at the ChessViewer repository root to recreate the local native/
workspace. The native/ folder itself is intentionally not tracked in Git.
These are upstream's terms; Popeye is not relicensed under ChessViewer's AGPL.

The historical GPL notice on SourceForge does not describe the current terms:
https://github.com/thomas-maeder/popeye/issues/204

Rebuild instructions and the exact compiler flags are in
native/popeye/build.sh inside the build-inputs archive. The script accepts
either a checkout of the upstream revision or the extracted upstream source
archive beside it.

The Emscripten runtime license is included in EMSCRIPTEN-LICENSE.txt.
